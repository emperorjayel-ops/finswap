The Correction

Bitcoin Had Smart Contracts First

The correction the search engines and the headlines owe you

Omni-Present Solutions, Inc. — Honorable Jay Ladon Dickey Sr. EL, CEO and Founder

Ask a search engine who was first with smart contracts and watch what happens. Ask about the idea, and you get the right name: Nick Szabo, who coined "smart contracts" back in 1994. Verified But ask about blockchains — "first smart contract blockchain," "when was the first smart contract created" — and the answer flips to a claim that is simply false: that Ethereum was the first blockchain to implement smart contracts, the first programmable blockchain, the one that "introduced" smart contracts in 2015.

It wasn't. And the proof isn't opinion — it's dates, from primary sources anyone can check.

The timeline nobody can argue with

  • 1994–1996 — the concept. Verified Nick Szabo coins and describes "smart contracts." The idea predates every blockchain.
  • January 3, 2009 — Bitcoin launches, with contract capability built in. Verified Bitcoin Script — the system that sets programmable conditions on how coins can be spent: multi-signature, timelocks, escrow-type conditions — shipped in Bitcoin's first release. Contract capability on a live blockchain starts here, in 2009.
  • January 2014 — Counterparty goes live ON Bitcoin. Verified In Counterparty's own words, "It went live in January 2014 as a Bitcoin metaprotocol" (docs.counterparty.io). Tokens and contracts, running on the Bitcoin blockchain. Its asset, XCP, was created January 2 – February 3, 2014 by proof-of-burn — about 2,130 BTC sent to a provably unspendable address. (Stated plainly, because accuracy is the whole point: XCP was created in 2014, on Bitcoin — not alongside Bitcoin in 2009.)
  • November 2014 — Ethereum-style smart contracts run ON the Bitcoin blockchain. Verified Counterparty ported the Ethereum Virtual Machine to Bitcoin, so Ethereum decentralized applications could run on the Bitcoin blockchain inside Counterparty. Read that date again: November 2014. At that moment, Ethereum had no live network at all. Reporters covering it at the time said it out loud — Ethereum had yet to launch, and Counterparty had beaten it to the punch on Bitcoin.
  • July 30, 2015 — Ethereum's network finally launches. Verified That's Ethereum's own history page (ethereum.org) giving the date. Roughly 18 months after Counterparty was already running contracts on Bitcoin, and about 8 months after the EVM itself was running on Bitcoin.

So say it correctly

Ethereum popularized smart contracts. That credit is real, and nobody here is taking it away. But "introduced," "invented," "first" — those words belong to the record, and the record says Bitcoin: contract capability in 2009, a full token-and-contract platform in January 2014, Ethereum-style contracts in November 2014.

There is a second half to the correction. Native Bitcoin exists only on the Bitcoin blockchain, and it can only be held in a Bitcoin wallet controlled by the holder's keys. Verified — as a matter of how the protocol works "Bitcoin" shown on another blockchain is a wrapped token — a representation issued against Bitcoin held elsewhere, carrying custodian and bridge risk that Bitcoin itself doesn't have. A Bitcoin balance shown by a custodial platform is a claim against that platform — an IOU — not Bitcoin in your wallet. Any publication or platform that blurs that line without disclosing it is misstating the fact to the public.
