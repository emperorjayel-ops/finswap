ISO 20022-Ready ✓

FinSwap uses ISO 20022-ready, ISO 20022-aligned messaging. ISO 20022 is the international standard for financial messaging — a common, data-rich language for payments and settlements between financial institutions. It is the standard behind SWIFT's cross-border CBPR+ programme (coexistence with legacy MT messages ended November 22, 2025) and the U.S. Fedwire Funds Service migration (July 14, 2025). FinSwap formats its payment, card-load, and API messages to this standard, so a FinSwap instruction carries structured sender, beneficiary, amount, and remittance data that banks and payment systems can read directly. This readiness is about the messaging layer — how instructions are written and exchanged — which is exactly what ISO 20022 governs.

Counterparty — Assets on Bitcoin, Since 2014

The layer under XCP, PERFECTCERTS, WEALTHTICKET, and BRICSCOIN on FinSwap — researched from Counterparty's public documentation.

  • A layer on Bitcoin, not its own blockchain. Counterparty launched in January 2014 — before Ethereum — as a protocol layer on top of Bitcoin. It has no separate chain and no miners of its own.
  • How it works. Counterparty embeds its protocol messages inside ordinary Bitcoin transactions (via OP_RETURN data). Counterparty nodes read those Bitcoin transactions and maintain the asset ledger — who created, holds, sends, and trades each asset — and that ledger is secured by Bitcoin's proof-of-work.
  • Anyone can issue an asset inside Bitcoin. Named assets run 4–12 uppercase characters and cost a 0.5 XCP fee that is burned; numeric assets are free. Assets can be divisible to 8 decimal places, and holders can create, send, and trade assets and pay distributions (dividends in BTC, XCP, or another asset) in a decentralized, trustless way — including on Counterparty's built-in decentralized exchange, running since 2014.
  • Early proof it worked. Spells of Genesis (2015) and the Rare Pepe collectibles (2016) were among the first collectible tokens anywhere — issued on Bitcoin through Counterparty, years before NFTs were a household word.

How the Currency Works — On Bitcoin, For Bitcoin

Plain mechanics, so anyone can follow the money.

  • Bought on the Bitcoin blockchain. You send Bitcoin on the Bitcoin blockchain to the public address above, or swap BTC inside a Counterparty wallet, and receive WEALTHTICKET, PERFECTCERTS, BRICSCOIN, or XCP in that same wallet.
  • Exchangeable for Bitcoin because they ride Bitcoin. These assets are issued and transferred inside Bitcoin transactions via Counterparty. Trading them for BTC happens on Counterparty's built-in decentralized exchange or wallet-to-wallet — no separate chain, no bridge.
  • Secured by Bitcoin. Every transfer is a Bitcoin transaction confirmed by Bitcoin proof-of-work. Ownership is the on-chain record — check any asset or balance yourself on xcp.io.
  • Spendable on everything here. Any listing — development, home, car, land, gold, silver, service, HIIYAH telecom and eSIM plan, Dominion Network media and streaming, Excalibur Line journey or pass, or Energy Source generator — can be priced and settled in BTC, XCP, WEALTHTICKET, PERFECTCERTS, BRICSCOIN, or physical Goldbacks. Sellers can hold, swap back to BTC, or trade onward.
  • Other currencies welcome. Currencies that exchange for Bitcoin can come in, convert to BTC on the Bitcoin blockchain, and then into any FinSwap asset or purchase.
  • Private & decentralized by design. Deals are wallet-to-wallet between the parties. FinSwap never holds your keys. Real Swaps and offers settle for real through your own wallet and xcp.io — worldwide.
