About this app

FinSwap is a Bitcoin and Counterparty asset app by OMNI-PRESENT SOLUTIONS, INC.: a self-custody wallet view, swaps, card loads, and trading of real-world asset listings. In-app trading is a demo preview until a live wallet integration connects.

What data the app handles

  • Self-custody: FinSwap is self-custody. Your keys stay on your device under your control; we never receive, transmit, or store your private keys or seed phrase. Never share your seed phrase with anyone, including us.
  • Wallet addresses: a public Bitcoin address is public information on the Bitcoin blockchain. The app displays a receive QR code for the operator's published Bitcoin address. When you scan-to-send, signing is handed to your own wallet app; this app does not sign transactions for you.
  • Session data: demo trading and preview activity are session-only on your device and are not stored on our servers.
  • External services: blockchain explorers and reference sites linked from the app (for example blockstream.info and counterparty.io) have their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data to anyone.
  • We do not run third-party advertising networks or ad trackers in this app.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, call logs, or device identifiers for advertising.

Payments and financial features

The wallet receive QR code is real: it encodes the operator's published Bitcoin address, and any Bitcoin sent to it moves on the public Bitcoin blockchain. In-app trading, swaps, and card loads are demo only until a live wallet integration connects — no funds move through demo features.

Data retention

We do not operate a server-side account database for this app. Blockchain transactions are public and permanent by the nature of the Bitcoin network and cannot be deleted by us.

Children

This app is directed to users 18 and over. Cryptocurrency involves risk; nothing in the app is financial advice.
